At first, no investment was needed

From day one, the founder paid the rent from personal capital, which was enough for the first 5 months. Winter went well. After the test period, the partner left, the official opening never happened, and the founder's money ran out. The store delayed payments to brands, the brands stopped bringing items, and sales fell almost to zero.

  1. 2025
    The founder invests personal money: the building lease, renovation, interior and furniture. The partner was inspired by the idea of a stylish multi-brand clothing store and took on all operations: brands, team, sales. The store was planned as an investment, and the founder was not involved in day-to-day management.
  2. October 2025 - January 2026
    The store opens in October with almost no advertising, no clear signage and no official opening. From November to January it sells $20,100-27,800 a month.
  3. February 2026
    The partner leaves the project. All management goes too, because brands, team and sales depended on the partner. The official opening of the store never takes place. The founder's money for rent runs out.
  4. Spring and summer 2026
    Several hired managers in a row, all unsuccessful. Payments to brands are delayed, and the brands stop bringing in goods. The shelves empty out, and revenue falls to $4,100 in August.
  5. October 2026
    After the failure with hired managers, the founder takes over management and brings in a new manager as an assistant, someone the founder has known and trusted for a long time. The manager's task is to build a system and take the store to results above the previous level. We will introduce the team and the manager when you first reach out, on Zoom or in person.

How the deal is structured

A contract with the company that owns the store and the lease rights to the building. The investor's money goes straight to where it is needed, not into the store's operating account.

Company

PT Maison Sauvage Bali, a company with foreign capital (PT PMA). The contract with the investor is signed by the company.

Contract form

We choose it together with the investor. The payout terms stay the same: 80% of net profit until payback, then 15% until the end of the lease.

We pay directly

The remaining rent goes directly to the landlord, and debts to brands go directly to the brands after reconciliation. A receipt for every payment.

Documents

In Indonesian and English, under Indonesian law, with a notary where required. The store pays for preparing the documents. The investor may bring their own lawyer.

The landlord confirms

The landlord has already signed the new payment schedule. Before the first payment, the landlord confirms in writing that the contract is in force and consents to the company subletting the building with the rent paid to the investor. Without this letter, we do not transfer money.

Oversight

Every month, a report on profit and payout, plus bank and card terminal statements. Access to daily sales staff reports.

The investor's sublease right

If the investor receives less than $70,000 for 2027, the investor gets the right to have the building sublet to a tenant of their choice and recover the investment from the rent.

When it applies

If the investor receives less than $70,000 for 2027. First, the store has 60 days to pay the difference.

What the investor gets

The company sublets the building, as a whole or by floor, to a tenant chosen by the investor, until 1 July 2030. The rent goes to the investor until the outstanding balance is repaid. Anything above that stays with the company.

Furniture and equipment

Pledged to the investor as collateral, under an inventory list. Bought for $39,000, worth less used. If the right is triggered, they can be sold toward the outstanding balance.

Protection for the store

Until then, the investor has no right to occupy the building. The threshold is calculated from bank and terminal statements. The company can repay the outstanding balance at any time and remove this right.

What the remaining lease is worth

The prepaid lease is a right to use the building, not ownership. As of 1 January 2028, its remaining value at the contract price is about $118,000. How much a sublease brings in depends on the market.

Landlord's consent

This is a condition of the deal. The landlord gives written consent before the first payment, in an addendum to the lease agreement. Without it, the contract does not allow subletting the whole building.
